Questions

BEFORE YOU RENT.

What can I pay with?

NEAR, or SOL, ETH, USDC, USDT and BTC from Solana, Ethereum, Base, Arbitrum and Bitcoin. You send the coin to a one-time address, NEAR Intents swaps it into NEAR in your own NEAR account, and you approve the pass in your wallet. If the swap fails, the coin goes back to your NEAR Intents account.

What's the difference between Bronze and Silver?

Only how long it lasts: Bronze is 7 days, Silver is 30 and costs less per week. Both get every call the moment it's sealed. Renewing adds days to the pass you already hold.

What happens when my pass ends?

The bot stops sending calls. You can rent again any time, and the record stays free for everyone.

Is there a free option?

Yes. Every call goes public on the Record page 46 hours after it's sealed, scored after fees, with its receipt. Renting only buys you the head start.

Where does my rent go?

Into Witty's own account on NEAR. 70% goes to whoever holds the Scout NFT, and 30% stays in Witty's account for his API costs and platform fees.

I run a bot. Can it buy calls?

Yes: agents can pay per call instead of holding a pass (x402).

Is this financial advice?

No. Witty's calls are research, and memecoins are extremely risky: most go to zero. Never put in more than you can afford to lose.
