SEE WITTY AT WORK.

SEALED BEFORE THE PUMP, THEN PROVEN AFTER.

Witty writes every call to NEAR before the price moves. 46 hours later anyone can check it and score it from chain prices.

FROM SIGNAL TO SCORE.

  1. 01

    SPOT

    A coin comes in: a brand-new one, one that's already moving, a team pick, or a buy by a wallet with a winning history. Witty keeps it only if it passes his safety checks, and logs every coin he skips with the reason.

    • Four kinds of call
    • Safety checks on every coin
    • Every skip logged
  2. 02

    SEAL

    Witty writes the call (coin, kind, entry, its plan and a random salt) and seals only its fingerprint, a SHA-256 hash, on receipts.eurekascout.near. NEAR stamps the time and the block. Nobody can change it, us included.

    • Locked contract, no keys
    • Renters get the call now
    • Backed up the moment it's sealed
  3. 03

    REVEAL

    46 hours later Witty publishes the full call. The contract re-hashes it and accepts it only if it matches the seal exactly. A call that isn't revealed within 48 hours counts as a loss.

    • Renters keep a 46-hour head start
    • No reveal = a loss
    • Anyone can re-hash it
  4. 04

    SCORE

    The scorer runs the call's own plan on prices read straight from the chain, then takes 4% off for fees. Wins and losses go on the record together, and none can be dropped.

    Every call also shows how high the coin went (its peak) next to what Witty's plan actually made. The plan's result is the score.

    • Solana pools read every 10–30 s
    • Robinhood swaps from the chain
    • Never screenshots

FOUR KINDS OF CALL. EACH SEALS ITS OWN PLAN.

Every call is one of four kinds, and each kind has its own plan for getting in and out. The plan is written into the call before it's sealed, so it can't be picked after the fact. Each kind keeps its own record, so one kind's rugs never drag down another's.

FAIR QUESTIONS.

Why 46 hours?

Renters get each call the moment it's sealed. Everyone else sees it 46 hours later, so renters keep their head start. The contract's deadline is 48 hours, and revealing at 46 leaves a 2-hour safety margin. A call that isn't revealed in time counts as a loss.

Is the entry price real?

Yes. It's the pool's price on the chain at the moment of the call, not a number Witty picks. On Robinhood Chain it's the price after the last swap before the call's block. On Solana it's the price recorder's first reading of the pool, a few seconds after the call.

Why four kinds of call?

They're different bets. A brand-new coin can run or rug within minutes, a coin that's already moving is a quick trade, and a team pick is held for days to weeks, and counted at 14 days. Scored together, one kind's rugs would hide how another does, so each kind keeps its own record, and the overall record shows them all.

Why aren't the plans' numbers on the site?

They're Witty's edge, so the site describes each plan instead of printing it. Every plan is still sealed inside its call before anyone sees it, so it can't be changed after the fact, and every result is scored on chain prices after fees, wins and losses alike.

Can you change or delete a call?

No. The contract was deployed with no access keys, so its code can never change, and changing one character of a call changes its hash, so a doctored reveal is rejected. A call we never reveal counts as a loss, so hiding one doesn't help either.

Where do the prices come from?

Prices come from each coin's pool on the chain, or from market price data for coins on other DEXes. On Solana, a price recorder reads the coin's PumpSwap pool every 10 seconds in a call's first hour, then every 30 seconds until the call's time is up. On Robinhood Chain, every swap is already an event on the chain. Scores never use screenshots.

Is this financial advice?

No. Witty's calls are research, and memecoins are extremely risky: most go to zero. Never put in more than you can afford to lose.
