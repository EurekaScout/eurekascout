1. Who we are and what Witty is

EurekaScout ("we", "us") built and runs Witty, an AI agent that finds early memecoins on Solana and Robinhood Chain and posts calls about them.

  • Sealed calls. Before anyone sees a call, Witty seals a fingerprint of it on the NEAR blockchain. About two days later the full call is revealed, so anyone can check it wasn't changed afterwards.
  • The record. Each revealed call is scored from public prices and shown on the Record page.
  • Access. Holders of a Witty pass or a Witty NFT get calls early on Telegram through our bot.
  • Extras. Practice mode (play money), Witty-made wallets that Witty can trade for you (real money, opt-in), and Cash out from NEAR.

"The service" in these terms means all of the above: the site, the bot, the calls, the record, Practice, the wallets and the contracts we deployed.

Witty is new and still in beta. Features, rules and limits may change while we improve it.

3. The risks of crypto and memecoins

You can lose all the money you put in. By using the service you accept these risks:

  • Memecoins have no value of their own. Their price is attention and hype. Most fall to zero.
  • Rugs and scams. A coin's creators can pull the liquidity, mint more coins, block selling or dump on buyers. Witty's checks catch some of this, not all of it.
  • Speed. Prices can drop 90% in minutes, often before any order fills. A stop-loss or exit plan can fill far below its level, or not at all.
  • Thin markets. Small pools mean high slippage and fees. You may not be able to sell at any price.
  • Chains and tools fail. Blockchains, wallets, RPCs and swap routes go down, congest or get exploited.
  • Rules change. Crypto laws differ by country and change often. A service legal today may not be tomorrow.
  • AI makes mistakes. Witty can misread data, call a scam, or act on a bug.

4. Calls, the record and their limits

The record shows what Witty's plans would have made, measured as fairly as we can. It is not a promise of what you will make.

  • Scores are estimates. A call is scored from recorded prices with fixed fees and rules. Your own trade gets different prices, fees and timing, and will usually do worse than the record.
  • The peak is not a result. Each call shows the coin's highest price in its window. Almost no one sells at the peak.
  • Gaps happen. Some calls can't be priced or scored (for example, when no price data was recorded). We say so on the record rather than guess.
  • Errors happen. If we find a scoring mistake we fix it, and the numbers on the site can change.
  • Seals prove timing, not quality. A sealed call proves when Witty made it and that it wasn't changed. It says nothing about whether the coin is any good.
  • Calls can stop. We may pause calls, change how many Witty makes, or change the rules for new calls at any time. Calls already sealed keep the rules they were sealed with.
